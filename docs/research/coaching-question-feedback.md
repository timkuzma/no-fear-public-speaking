# Coaching questions and useful text feedback

Reviewed on 13 October 2026. The runtime uses condensed application-owned guidance in `server/coaching_context.py`, not entire articles or a retrieval service.

## Primary sources and adaptation

- [ICF PCC assessment criteria](https://coachingfederation.org/credentialing/performance-evaluations/criteria-for-assessing-pcc/): follow the speaker's words and goals; ask clear open questions one at a time that invite further thought. Adapted to distinct questions about an assertion's basis, conditions, meaning or consequences. Coaching certification standards do not certify this product or its AI.
- [Toastmasters evaluator role](https://www.toastmasters.org/membership/club-meeting-roles/evaluator) and [effective evaluation guidance](https://www.toastmasters.org/Magazine/Magazine%20Issues/2018/July2018/Sidebars/Evaluate%20Effectively): consider the speaker's purpose, give specific positive and constructive feedback. Adapted to evidence-linked strengths and actionable improvements beyond an optional user focus. No compulsory project pitch structure is inferred.
- [CCL SBI/SBII](https://www.ccl.org/articles/leading-effectively-articles/closing-the-gap-between-intent-vs-impact-sbii/): describe observable behavior in context and inquire about intent instead of assuming it. Adapted to exact text excerpts, possible comprehension effects, and a concrete editing action. Actual audience impact, psychological confidence, tone and gestures are not observable in this text pipeline.

## Product rules

Read the whole speech and its saved answers. Requested criteria add attention and never replace general analysis. Select evidence before making a claim. A useful recommendation explains the observed wording, the possible comprehension difficulty, and what the speaker can change or check without inventing their facts. Vague focus labels do not establish a pass/fail norm. Ordinary introductory words are not automatically mistakes.

Questions should develop the actual thought, avoid repeating answered facts, and contain one mental task. Use different relevant directions instead of three paraphrases. Do not import business metrics into a personal story or require a speaker to defend an invented premise.

These are design constraints, not measured guarantees. Structural evidence checks cannot replace independent semantic evaluation; see [validation](../feedback23-validation.md).
